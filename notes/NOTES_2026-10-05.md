# OpenReader — engineering log, 2026-10-05

## Contents' Downloaded check: one pass costs 0.65 ms over 2,076 rows, the per-row rule 40 ms (2026-10-05 01:27)

Measured in Node 22.23.2 with `tsx`, on a synthetic plan shaped like 仙逆's navigation: 13 volume headings and 2,063 chapters, 2,076 Contents rows, every chapter complete, mean of 20 runs after one warm-up. `downloadedRows`, which matches the rows to the plan by spine item and fragment and asks `downloadedChapters` once, took 0.65 ms; `downloadedChapters` alone took 0.13 ms. The rule the Download drawer used per row, `descendants(chapters, id)` and then `every(complete)`, took 39.96 ms for all 2,076 rows (mean of 3), because each call walks the whole plan. The script was a throwaway and was not kept: to repeat it, build that plan (each volume heading a part of its own with three sentences, each chapter a part of its own with sixty), a Contents row pointing at each part, a progress in which every chapter is complete, and time the three calls. Hermes on the phone was not measured; it interprets rather than compiles, so both numbers are larger there, and the ratio, about sixty times, is the point.
