# Contents shows which chapters are downloaded

## What was decided

In the reader, Contents marks every chapter that is downloaded with the check
the Download drawer already uses, at the right of its row, and nothing beside
it: the check is the word, as it is there.

- **For the voice in the player.** A chapter counts as downloaded in the voice
  Play would read it in, so the check means that this chapter plays from the
  phone, without a network and without cost. Choosing another voice changes the
  checks; with no voice chosen there are none.
- **Only a whole chapter.** A chapter with only some of its sentences saved, or
  one a download is still writing, shows nothing. Its progress stays in the
  Download drawer, where it can be paused.
- **A volume, when all of it is.** A volume's heading carries the check once
  every chapter under it with something to speak is downloaded, as in the
  Download drawer.
- **Never a page with nothing to say.** A cover, a page that is only a picture,
  or a row that cannot be opened never carries the check, even in a book that is
  downloaded from end to end.
- **As it happens.** With Contents open, a chapter's check appears when a
  download saves its last sentence, and goes when its audio is deleted. Contents
  opens as quickly as it did; the checks follow a moment later, once the app has
  read what is saved. If what is saved cannot be read, Contents shows no checks
  and says nothing, because the Download drawer already says what is wrong.

A change to the bracket setting does to the checks what it already does in the
Download drawer: a chapter with sentences whose spoken form the change alters
stops being downloaded until those sentences are downloaded again, and loses its
check; a chapter the change does not touch keeps it.

## Why

Contents is where the owner decides where to go next, and before this it could
not say which chapters would play without a network. To find out, the owner had
to leave it, open the book's actions, go to Download and read the checks there,
then come back. Before a trip, or on one, that is the question asked most.

## Alternatives turned down

- **Downloaded in any voice.** A chapter saved in one voice would carry the
  check while the player was set to another. Play would then fetch it over the
  network, and charge for it, under a check that said it would not.
- **The download ring in Contents too, for a chapter being written.** It would
  show progress without leaving Contents. But in the Download drawer the ring is
  a button that pauses the chapter; in Contents the same circle would do
  nothing, or would open the chapter, and one picture would mean two things.
- **A mark for a partly saved chapter.** It would say a little more. But a
  partly saved chapter still needs the network for the rest, and a mark that is
  half true is the one the owner would trust at the wrong moment.
- **The phone's own downloaded symbol, an arrow in a circle, instead of the
  check.** It is what the phone's Music app draws. But the Download drawer
  already says downloaded with the check, and the same fact would then look
  different in two drawers of one app. A check in Contents could be read as
  "already read", but the app marks nothing as read, so nothing in it can be
  confused with the check.
- **Dimming the chapters that are not downloaded.** Contents already dims the
  rows that cannot be opened, and the two meanings would run together.
- **A check on a cover or a picture page in a book that is downloaded
  throughout,** so that the book looks complete. It would claim audio for a page
  that has none.

## What it costs

The checks arrive a moment after Contents first opens on a book; until then the
list looks as it always did. Waiting for them would
have made every opening slower, in a list whose whole purpose is getting
somewhere quickly.
