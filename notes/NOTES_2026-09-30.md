## The phone's own bar titles grow with the text size; the app's stay at 17, because react-native-screens fixes the font (2026-09-30 11:53)

On the owner's iPhone 16 Pro, at a larger text size, the Library and Settings bar titles stayed the same size while the rest of the app grew (owner's screenshots, 11:37). Measured on `iPhone 17 share95` (iOS 27.0) with `xcrun simctl ui UDID content_size`, reading the height of the title's first capital in 3× screenshots:

| | `large` (default) | `extra-extra-extra-large` |
| --- | --- | --- |
| iOS's own Settings › General › About, "About" | A 36 px (17 pt) | A 44 px (about 21 pt) |
| OpenReader "Library", `headerTitleStyle: { color }` only | — | L 36 px (17 pt) |
| OpenReader "Library", `headerTitleStyle: { ...TEXT.headline, color }` (beta66) | L 36 px | — |

The two OpenReader shots are pixel-identical in the title (163 px wide, 2180 dark pixels): `[UIFont boldSystemFontOfSize:]` is the semibold system font, the same as beta66's `fontWeight: '600'`. So the phone's inline bar title does follow the text size, which contradicts ADR 0057's "the phone's own inline bar title does not follow Dynamic Type", and the app's never did.

Cause: react-native-screens 4.26.2, `ios/RNSScreenStackHeaderConfig.mm` 410–434. If any of `titleFontFamily`, `titleFontSize`, `titleFontWeight` or `titleColor` is set, it sets `titleTextAttributes` with a font of `titleFontSize ?: DEFAULT_TITLE_FONT_SIZE` (17): `[RCTFont updateFont:… scaleMultiplier:1.0]` when a family or weight is given, `[UIFont boldSystemFontOfSize:]` otherwise. Neither scales. The app has passed `titleColor` since #29, so its bar titles have been fixed at 17 since then.

Aside: changing the text size while the app ran left the Library row's progress line half cut off (its row kept its old height) until the app was restarted.

## react-native-screens drops the fraction of a bar title's font size (2026-09-30 12:15)

For #100.
