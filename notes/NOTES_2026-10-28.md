**Result: FAIL at the first phase-1 check; stopped at the bounded checkpoint.**

- **Defect (deterministic): a cold ordinary Library shows a standing search
  field with a magnifier, visible and hittable with no interaction — design
  0090 requires neither a field nor a magnifier during ordinary browsing, and
  the field must appear only on pull-down or `…` › Search.** Reproduces on the
  empty Library and with a Document present, across cold relaunches
  (`shots/p1-cold-library-empty.jpg`, `p1-cold-library-1row.jpg`). The
  independent XCUITest path asserts the contract and fails:
  `testColdLibraryShowsNoSearchField` — "found one (frame: {{16, 117},
  {370, 44}})" plus "must not be visible/tappable" (2 failures,
  `test-20261028-125759-58837.log`). Cause at code level: `hideWhenScrolling`
  outside search maps to `hidesSearchBarWhenScrolling` (`RNSScreenStackHeaderConfig.mm:607`), whose UIKit
  semantics start the bar visible at scroll top and hide it only after a
  scroll-down; nothing collapses it on appear. Every Library/Folder page
  shares this presentation.
- What passed on the same build with real XCUITest touches:
  `testTappingFieldFocusesAndTypes` (tap focuses; typed `Short` read back
  from the field; fragment match kept the fixture row) and
  `testActionsMenuSearchFocusesDirectly` (`…` › Search reaches input with no
  second tap; `zzzz-no-match` shows `No documents found`) — 0 failures each.
  So tap-to-focus, the direct menu entry, live filtering and the no-match
  message work; the defect is the initial presentation only, as far as
  measured.
- Harness trap met: mobilebuildmcp/AXe synthetic taps and `type-text`
  reported success while nothing landed (even a control tap on a Library row),
  with `snapshot_ui` answering a complete tree; recorded in
  `pitfalls/mcp.md` (2026-10-28). All interaction evidence above is
  XCUITest, not synthetic touches. One `sim_volume` reset to 60 between
  checks with nothing played (the known reset), re-silenced.
