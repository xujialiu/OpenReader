## Fabric resolves a border's dynamic colour against the process's trait collection, not the view's (2026-09-24 13:10)

Read out of the installed React Native 0.86.3, `React/Fabric/Mounting/ComponentViews/View/RCTViewComponentView.mm`, `invalidateLayer`. The background is `[_backgroundColor resolvedColorWithTraitCollection:self.traitCollection]`. A uniform border is `layer.borderColor = RCTUIColorFromSharedColor(…).CGColor`, and a non-uniform one (`borderBottom…`, `borderTop…`) goes through `RCTGetBorderImage`, which fills with `borderColors.*.CGColor`. Neither resolves against the view: `-[UIColor CGColor]` on a dynamic colour answers for `UITraitCollection.currentTraitCollection`, which UIKit sets to the view's own traits only inside its trait-aware callbacks. `traitCollectionDidChange:` calls `invalidateLayer` when the colour appearance changed, so that call is right; a call from a Fabric mount (a layout or props change) resolves against the process's, which follows the phone, not the window's `overrideUserInterfaceStyle`. The old architecture's `React/Views/RCTView.m` resolves all four border colours against `self.traitCollection` (lines 780–785); the Fabric view does not. `facebook/react-native` `main` on this date is the same (`layer.borderColor = borderColor.CGColor`, line 1152).
