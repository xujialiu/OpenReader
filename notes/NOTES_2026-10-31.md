New guard-regression probes, also 0 failures on both builds:

- `testDrawerOneFingerTapToggles`.
- `testDrawerTapsAfterShortSweeps`: 30 of 30 read-backs equal the model, taps 0.15 s and 1.5 s after the lift, both orders.
- `testDrawerTapWithin120msOfSweep`: 6 of 6, a third finger inside the sweep's own event record. The 300 ms version would have dropped these.

Four runs failed on the probe, each passing on the same build once the probe was corrected: one `testPlainTapsOpenAfterSweeps`, because a relaunch reopened the Folder browsed last; three `testDrawerFoldArrows`, from a stale tap point, fold state read only inside the viewport, and the drawer opening at the chapter being read. An `AXPress` through Xcode 27's Device Hub after a sweep did pass the guard on both lists. It arrives with a touch, so it says nothing of the assistive technologies themselves.

Correction to the 23:05 entry: "where the first presentation opens varies" was the probe, not the app. A passed run leaves the app on the drawer, so the next run's `openDrawer()` goes Back and Download, a remount, and a remount opens at the chapter being read. On one Debug build the leftover drawer began `Terminology, being read`, and a fresh launch began `Part 1`. Relaunch before each such run (`pitfalls/verification-runs.md`).
