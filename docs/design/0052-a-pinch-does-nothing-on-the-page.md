# A pinch does nothing on the page

_A product decision with no engineering half: the owner judged it too easy to
reverse to need one. Issue #79. The measurements are in the engineering log
for 2026-09-28._

## What changed

Two fingers pinched apart on the page used to magnify the whole page, as a
web page grows in the phone's browser. The text did not reflow to fit. It grew
past both edges of the screen: at the size one pinch reached, the chapter's
heading read "The First C" and every line ran off the right side. The page
stayed that way until the owner pinched it back. A double tap on the page never
magnified it.

Now the page is never magnified. A pinch leaves the page exactly as it was.
**Font Size** stays the one way to make the text bigger. It reflows the text
to the width of the screen, so the line being spoken, the highlight and a
tapped sentence all stay where they are meant to be.

## A pinch does nothing else either

Once the page could no longer grow, a pinch still did one thing: it took the
page off the voice. Fingers moving on the page are how the owner looks
somewhere else while the reading stays where it is (**Browsing**). So a pinch
switched the player's A to M, and while the page was being read aloud it
stopped following the line being spoken until the owner tapped M.

The owner decided that a pinch has no effect at all. From the moment a second
finger lands until every finger has lifted, the page takes no notice: it does
not start browsing, and a page moving to the next line keeps moving. One finger
dragging the page is Browsing, exactly as before.

## What was given up

- **Pictures, tables and formulas can no longer be enlarged.** Font Size makes
  text bigger and leaves pictures as they are, so a small figure in a Document
  stays small. The phone's own accessibility Zoom is untouched, and magnifies
  anything on the screen, this page included.
- **A two-finger drag does not count as browsing.** The phone lets two fingers
  scroll the page as one does. The app now takes any gesture with two fingers
  for a pinch, so a page scrolled that way while it follows the voice is
  brought back to the line being spoken at the next line.
- **Fingers that begin as one are one, until the second lands.** A pinch
  whose first finger has already dragged the page further than a tap by the
  time the second lands has started Browsing by then, as a one-finger drag
  would.

## What was turned down

- **A pinch that changes Font Size.** Pinching apart would step the size up
  and pinching together would step it down, as some reading apps do. It was
  turned down as a second feature, not a fix: the owner asked for the pinch to
  go away, and Font Size already has its place in Appearance, where the size is
  shown as a number. It could come later as a request of its own.
- **Leaving the pinch as Browsing.** It kept the rule simple: any finger that
  moves on the page takes the page off the voice. It lost because the owner
  wanted a pinch to do nothing, and an accidental pinch while listening would
  still have stopped the page following the voice.
