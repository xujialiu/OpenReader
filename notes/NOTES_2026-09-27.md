These measurements are accessibility disappearance waits, not physical touch-to-dismiss latency; the accompanying `tapElapsed` values (0.667–1.405 s) include XCTest's touch and synchronization overhead. The run passed with `Executed 1 test, with 0 failures` in 21.722 s. A React Fiber walk through the connected debug target reported `LookupDrawer` with the selection before the tap and `null` after it; the next observed drawer was caused by the test's explicit long-press reopen. The app close path therefore did not reproduce as an application defect in this controlled run.
