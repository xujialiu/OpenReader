## The README's reading GIF, recorded on a new simulator (2026-10-09 11:51)

- **The setup**
  - The simulator was made for this recording: "OpenReader 147 README", iPhone 17 Pro Max, iOS 27.0, `A3449E22-10CD-4AF1-809F-C997AC6C1586`. It was silenced to `sim_volume=0`, and its status bar was overridden to 9:41.
  - The app was a Debug build of `xujialiu/in_app_purchase` at `7b039fd`, served by Metro on port 8082.
  - The book was https://www.gutenberg.org/ebooks/1342.epub3.images (24.8 MB, nine spine items). It was added through the harness.
  - The voice was Fish Audio's "Sarah" (`en/933563129e564b19a115bedd57b7406a`), at the default 1.50×, in the dark theme.
- **Reaching Chapter I**
  - Twice, a touch on the Contents row "Chapter I." (`ax.py touch`) left the page at the start of `1342-h-0`. Whether the touch landed during the sheet's slide-in was not established.
  - Harness seeks found the chapter: Utterance 395 is "T is a truth universally acknowledged". The drop cap "I" is an image, so the text starts at "T".
- **What played**
  - The consent alert took one Play: about 4 s of sound, then a pause.
  - The recording (`simctl io recordVideo`, h264, 1320 × 2868) ran 14.5 s. Playback filled 12 s of it, from Utterance 395 into 396, and was paused at once.
  - 12 s was what 8 s of moving highlight needed, with Fish's start-up before it.
- **The GIF**
  - 1.8 s to 9.8 s of the recording, 15 fps, scaled to 360 wide with a 128-colour palette and no dither.
  - The result, `assets/readme/reading.gif`, is 360 × 782, 120 frames and 1.60 MB.
  - At 320 wide with 64 colours and 12 fps it was 0.99 MB, but the text was softer.
- **Configuring Fish**
  - `OfflineFixProbe.testConfigureFishProvider` typed the key but left the provider off.
  - Test connection and a touch on the switch through `ax.py` enabled it (test/manual-test/pitfalls/providers-and-audio.md).
