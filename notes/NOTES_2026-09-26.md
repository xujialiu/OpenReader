## Every cue while playing was revealed, so a drag lasted until the next sentence (2026-09-26 12:04)

Read out of `src/app/use-reading.ts` at 48a1556: the clock's `onClip` passed `{ reveal: playIntent.current }`, true for every cue while the reading plays, and the renderer's `'speak'` branch ran `if (message.reveal) browsing = false;` before `follow(shown)`. So a page dragged away while listening came back at the next Clip's cue from wherever it was, by a glide within the visible page and otherwise by a jump or a `display()` of the reading's section. It agrees with the batch 1 drag probe (01:42 onwards): after each real drag, one or two line changes moved nothing (Browsing), and the next sentence's cue brought the page back. `engine.ts` also cues again while playing on `setRate` (`cue(last.clip)`) and on a Voice switch, and `play()` cues the queue's front synchronously, so the first cue after Play is the one `play()` itself causes when anything is queued.

## epub.js sets the scroll container's overflow through its Stage, once (2026-09-26 12:04)

Read out of the bundled `@epubjs-react-native/core` `lib/module/epubjs.js`. `Stage.create()` gives a vertical `scroll` Stage the inline `overflow-y: scroll; overflow-x: hidden`, and `Stage.overflow(t)` writes the same inline style (`overflow: t` for anything but `scroll`) and stores `t` in `settings.overflow`. Its one other caller is `DefaultViewManager.updateFlow` (the continuous manager's override passes `"scroll"`), reached only from `rendition.flow()`, which `Rendition.start()` runs once and the library's `changeFlow` runs on request; this app never calls `changeFlow`. `Stage.size()`, called on resize, does not touch overflow.
