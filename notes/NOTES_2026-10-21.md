## Expo writes CFBundleVersion as a literal at prebuild (2026-10-21 10:40)

`node_modules/@expo/config-plugins/build/ios/Version.js` maps `ios.buildNumber` to `CFBundleVersion` (`infoPlistProperty: 'CFBundleVersion'`, falling back to `'1'`). The main checkout's prebuilt `ios/OpenReader/Info.plist` holds `<key>CFBundleVersion</key>` `<string>9</string>`, not `$(CURRENT_PROJECT_VERSION)`. So an `xcodebuild` build setting cannot give the second archive of a pair another number; it needs its own prebuild (ADR 0083).
