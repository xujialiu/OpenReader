## The share sheet rises over the drawer's Modal, and the drawer is still there when it closes (2026-09-30 02:44)

For #95. `ios-tester` measured this on `iPhone 17 share95` (DCCC5DE7-26E4-4F9A-B92D-2998ECBDBB00, iOS 27.0), Debug `0.0.2-beta61` at `00f0b31`, Metro 8088, with `expo-sharing` 57.0.22 and no config plugin. The book was a 1 MB part of the owner's *The First Legendary Beast Master*.

- `Sharing.shareAsync` presented `UIActivityViewController` over the drawer's React Native `Modal`. The sheet's header read "The First Legendary Beast Maste… · Book · 1 MB", which is the Library title and not `sha256-…`, with an EPUB document icon. It offered OpenReader, More, Copy and Save to Files. With the sheet up, `app.debugDescription` still listed the drawer: `Close <title>`, the title, `Share` at (358, 604, 34, 34) marked `Disabled`, then Rename, Download and Delete. In a screenshot the sheet covers the drawer, which starts 604 pt down.
- When the sheet's Copy cell closed it, the drawer was still open, with Share at the same frame. The `ShareProbe` methods passed 5 of 5.
- In the Debug Log, `share 5ab3e9f0 as "<name>": copied in 4–13 ms` over several shares, and `sheet closed after 2589–3250 ms` (the tap to close was the probe's).
- `cmp` found the `Library/Caches/share/<name>.epub` copy identical to `Documents/library/sha256-5ab3e9f0….epub`. Both had the same SHA-256 as the original part in `~/Works/epub_books` (`161c4aa9…`).
- The long rename produced `The First Legendary Beast Master, Volume Three - The Long Road Through the Northern Mountains and Beyond.epub`. The drawer's title frame was (0, 560, 358, 94), four lines, with the button at (358, 560, 34, 34) beside the first.
- With the kept file moved out, the drawer showed the note "The file for this book is not on this device any more, so there is nothing to share. Add the book again to share it.", no sheet rose, and the Debug Log said `share 5ab3e9f0: no file`.
- The sheet's actions could not be driven from the Mac. `snapshot_ui` and AXe did not see them. XCUITest saw them as `Cell`s and not `Button`s. Save to Files took a raw tap once in about six tries, and the Files dialog's Save never did (`test/manual-test/pitfalls/mcp.md`).
