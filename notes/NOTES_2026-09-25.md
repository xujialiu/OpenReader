## Production queue reports output coordinates and retires buffers at output (2026-09-25 10:26)

The owner authorized implementation after the prototype comparison. The installed native queue, buffer processor and WSOLA now compile together in `test/native-audio/run.sh`; only scheduling/session/event/graph-ownership plumbing is adapted for the host. Lifecycle checks passed for pause/resume, clear/seek, exact content endpoints, tail drain, partially and fully pre-read removals for voice replacement, input arriving during starvation flush and rate changes. Buffers removed from the input list alone had already entered WSOLA, so production retains references until output and reconstructs retained lookahead when removal reaches pre-read content.

The actual queue rendered all 1,269 buffers of the real Dax chapter 179 at both 1.50× and 1.55×, delivered all 1,269 output-ended callbacks, and ended at exactly 327.683 content seconds. At 1.55× the final waveform run (`queue155-final`) accepted 697 matches and reported <0.1 ms start/middle/end median error, with no growth. Final output duration was 211.435 s. At 1.50× the earlier production-queue run was 218.512 s with 727 matches and <0.1 ms medians. The native tail criterion was then tightened from the prototype's fixed 50 ms source margin: output, overlap and the next search window must contain no coordinates preceding the content end before the final callback/reset. Padding never enters content duration.

The app now gives a seek a fresh source and callback generation and resets its timeline to zero, eliminating both cached DSP history and delayed old-source events. A drained section gets its first Clip cue immediately on enqueue rather than waiting for another buffer/position event. Section transitions drain the preceding tail and wait an additional 100 ms; pause retains the source. Section identity is a lazy callback from the reader, so newly rendered text is included and a remapped reading uses its current indices.

The native source-only patch applies through patch-package. The first iOS Debug rebuild returned exit 0; final native rebuild/installation and on-screen verification are delegated separately. The beta was chosen as 0.0.2-beta24 after checking main and all remaining worktrees. No physical phone installation has been performed. Full app, AirPods, background and renderer scheduling claims require their own evidence.
