## How `Intl.Segmenter` counts words, the plugin's count (2026-09-22 00:47)

Node 22.23.2, ICU 78.2, `new Intl.Segmenter('en', { granularity: 'word' })`, counting `isWordLike` segments. Between two letters a `:` joins (`a:b` 1), as do `.`, `'`, `’`, `‘`, `·`, U+2024 and U+FF0E (`e.g`, `U.S`, `rock'n'roll`, `hello·world` are one each); between two digits `.`, `,`, `;` and `'` join (`3.14`, `1,000`, `1;2`, `1'2000`) and `:` does not (`10:30` is 2). A letter and a digit side by side are one word (`v1.2.3` is 1), but `.` or `'` between a letter and a digit breaks (`a.1`, `1'a` are 2). `_` joins; `-`, `/`, `;` between letters, `+`, `$`, `%`, `&`, `#`, `@` and `=` all break or count nothing. `第一章`, `カタカナ`, `สวัสดี` and `한국어` are each **one** word, found by dictionary.

## The first Fish request after a quiet minute fails because the proxy drops idle HTTP/3 (2026-09-22 02:29)

Reproduced for #26 on this Mac with a Swift `URLSession` probe (not the simulator, which another run was using): request A, N seconds idle on the same session, then B and C. Zotero-TTS was idle, and most runs sent no key, so no account limit could apply.

- **HTTP/3, the default.** Fish's replies advertise `alt-svc: h3=":443"; ma=86400`, macOS remembers it, and from the second connection on every request went over HTTP/3. The app does the same: its connection store on the iPhone 17 simulator holds an HTTP/3 entry for `api.fish.audio`.
- **The threshold is 60 s.** Reusing a connection idle 20, 45, 50, 55 or 58 s worked (401 for a keyless POST in about 20 ms). All 9 reuses after 60–120 s failed. Each one stalled, sent two keep-alive probes a second apart, and gave up with `NSURLErrorDomain -1005 "The network connection was lost."` (`_kCFStreamErrorDomainKey=4, _kCFStreamErrorCodeKey=-4`) after 6.7–6.95 s. The next request, on a new connection, answered at once. macOS closes an idle HTTP/3 connection itself at about 120 s, so on macOS the window is about 60–120 s; the app failed at 206 s, so iOS keeps them longer.
- **A GET survives it and a POST does not.** CFNetwork retries a GET (`could retry(Y) … has forbidden protocols`) on a new HTTP/2 connection, which answered 200 after 7.1–7.2 s in total. A POST is not retried (`idempotent(N) … bytes written(Y) … can retry(N)`), and every synthesis is a POST.
- **A keyed synthesis fails the same way.** `s2.1-pro-free` with Dax, 100 s idle: -1005 after 6760 ms. The POST sent straight after it answered 200 in 335 ms with 5,893 bytes.
- **HTTP/2 over TCP is unaffected.** With an ephemeral configuration, whose first connection is HTTP/2, reuse after 45, 60, 90 and 200 s all worked.
- **The path.** Clash Verge Rev runs its mihomo core in TUN mode: `utun4` has 198.18.0.1, and `api.fish.audio` resolves to the fake address 198.18.0.126 with a 1 s TTL. Fish is routed direct: mihomo connects from 192.168.31.28 to Cloudflare at 104.18.1.100:443, and the replies carry a `cf-ray` ending `-HKG`. mihomo's outgoing UDP socket for a connection disappears about 60 s after its last packet. The next packet leaves from a new source port, and nothing comes back on it.

Not established: whether a phone behaves the same. Its trigger here is the proxy's 60 s UDP expiry, which a proxy app on the phone, or a router, could share. Also not established: why Cloudflare does not answer the new port. The probe's source and outputs stayed in the session's scratch directory.
