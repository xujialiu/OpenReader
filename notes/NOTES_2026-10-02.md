## The reading accent each word colour gives, and its contrast on the app's surfaces (2026-10-02 10:15)

For #118's accent (`src/app/accent.ts`, commit `9da5b84`). WCAG 2 contrast ratios, computed by the module itself under vitest. The accent is the word colour mixed in sRGB, channel by channel, towards black (light theme) or white (dark theme), searched in halves for the least mix that reads at 4.5:1 on every surface of its set. Two sets per theme:

- `reading`, on the app's own surfaces: light `#ffffff` and `#f4f4f6`; dark `#111114` and `#1c1c21`.
- `onMark`, on a drawer's marked row and its header's round button and capsule: light `#dcdce2` and `#ffffff`; dark `#3e3e47` and `#2c2c32`.
