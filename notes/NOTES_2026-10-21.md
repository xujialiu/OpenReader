## Expo writes CFBundleVersion as a literal at prebuild (2026-10-21 10:40)

`node_modules/@expo/config-plugins/build/ios/Version.js` maps `ios.buildNumber` to `CFBundleVersion` (`infoPlistProperty: 'CFBundleVersion'`, falling back to `'1'`). The main checkout's prebuilt `ios/OpenReader/Info.plist` holds `<key>CFBundleVersion</key>` `<string>9</string>`, not `$(CURRENT_PROJECT_VERSION)`. So an `xcodebuild` build setting cannot give the second archive of a pair another number; it needs its own prebuild (ADR 0083).

## The open Player rubber-banded under a drag at every size: React Native's default alwaysBounceVertical (2026-10-21 15:13)

For #312. Session-owned `iPhone 17 player312` (iOS 26.5, `C237AE52-161F-46C0-A72A-E0D50BEB2778`), a Debug app (`CFBundleVersion` 11, no native change since 8a5add9) re-signed for Metro 8312, the short fixture open, no audio.

- **The symptom, measured.** `player-drag.py` (`axe drag` 200 pt up over 4 s from a plain spot of the open Player, shots at 1.5/2.5/3.5 s): Play's circle -49.0, -48.7, -48.3 pt mid-drag with the `No provider is enabled.` note, -44.7 and -45.0 pt without it (from the head row and from the transport row), +0.0 after the finger lifted every time.
