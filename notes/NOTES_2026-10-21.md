## Expo writes CFBundleVersion as a literal at prebuild (2026-10-21 10:40)

`node_modules/@expo/config-plugins/build/ios/Version.js` maps `ios.buildNumber` to `CFBundleVersion` (`infoPlistProperty: 'CFBundleVersion'`, falling back to `'1'`). The main checkout's prebuilt `ios/OpenReader/Info.plist` holds `<key>CFBundleVersion</key>` `<string>9</string>`, not `$(CURRENT_PROJECT_VERSION)`. So an `xcodebuild` build setting cannot give the second archive of a pair another number; it needs its own prebuild (ADR 0083).

### Phase A — the timed runs

Method per `download-concurrency.md`, "Timing a chapter download on the
simulator": warm-up download of both chapters (untimed, 117 s, prepares their
text), then ABAB with `download-chapter.cjs`, each run started from an empty
clip directory with the app freshly relaunched (the in-memory clip cache,
pitfalls #134, serves a same-process re-download without requests). Between
runs: `deleteDownloaded(nav.102, nav.103)` through the runtime with the plan and
inventory loaded, then a relaunch. A window sampler
(`download-window-sampler.cjs`) read `task.state`, `task.writing` (change) /
`task.current` (baseline) and `occupied` every 250 ms through one CDP
