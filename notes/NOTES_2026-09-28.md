# OpenReader — engineering log, 2026-09-28

## A pinch magnifies the reading page 2.6×, and a double tap does not (2026-09-28 09:30)

`0.0.2-beta39` JavaScript from this worktree on Metro 8100, a new iPhone 17 simulator (iOS 27.0) holding a Debug app copied from another worktree's device, `A Short Test of Reading Aloud` open and paused. `test/manual-test/zoom.sh` (#79) makes each gesture through `ZoomProbe` in a freshly launched app, then asks the page for its own `visualViewport.scale` through the walkthrough harness.

- **Pinch** (`XCUIElement.pinch(withScale: 3, velocity: 2)` on the app, middle of the screen): `scale=2.6013922691345215 innerWidth=155`, with the viewport `width=device-width, initial-scale=1.0` from `@epubjs-react-native/core`'s `template.js`. The page stayed magnified: the heading read "The First C" and every line ran off the right edge. At 09:22, a pinch on a reader opened through the harness had left it at 2.1450889110565186, one `visualViewport` resize event.
- **Double tap** (a quarter of the way down, on the first sentence): `scale=1 innerWidth=402`. It moved the reading to the tapped sentence, which was highlighted, and magnified nothing.
- **Control, 09:20.** The same pinch on Safari, on a page served from the Mac with the same viewport, took its `visualViewport.scale` from 1.00 to 2.39.

So the library's `scrollEnabled: false` on the WebView does not stop a pinch from zooming it.

A pinch also switched the player's Following mark from A to M, before the change as after it. Its first finger moves more than `DRAG_PX`, and `dragged` in `highlighter.ts` counts that as Browsing (#71).

## The first readings said 1, because the pinch landed on the placeholder (2026-09-28 09:23)

Three `zoom.sh` runs before the one above read `scale=1` for the pinch as well. In each, the probe had tapped the Library row itself and pinched after waiting for "Laying the document out…" to go. Its `pinch-before` screenshot still showed that placeholder at 12 s, and at 29 s in a slower run. The wait had passed at once: `app.staticTexts` matching `label BEGINSWITH 'Laying the document out'` found nothing while the screenshot showed it, so the placeholder is not in the accessibility tree. Neither is the page's text: `app.webViews` matched nothing. `zoom.sh` now opens the Document through the harness and waits for Metro's `HX … rendered=N` line before each gesture.

## With the viewport limited, neither gesture magnifies (2026-09-28 09:36)

The same `zoom.sh`, after `patches/` added `maximum-scale=1.0, user-scalable=no` to the template's viewport and Metro was restarted with `--clear`. The bundle carried the new viewport once. Both gestures landed on laid-out text, as the screenshots show:

- pinch: `scale=1 innerWidth=402`;
- double tap: `scale=1 innerWidth=402`;
- the page's viewport read `width=device-width, initial-scale=1.0, maximum-scale=1.0, user-scalable=no`.

`npx patch-package @epubjs-react-native/core` ran for 120 s without writing the patch and was stopped. The two `template.js` hunks were made with `git diff --no-index` instead. Then `git apply -R` took the whole patch off `node_modules`, and `npx patch-package`, the `postinstall` step, applied it again: `@epubjs-react-native/core@1.4.8 ✔`.

## A pinch leaves the Following mark on A once `dragged` ignores two fingers (2026-09-28 09:52)

The owner decided that a pinch does nothing on the reading page, Browsing included. `highlighter.ts` now has `landed` on `touchstart`, in each section document and on the scroll container, which marks a gesture as a pinch once `touches.length > 1`. `dragged` returns before `halt()` for the rest of that gesture. `ZoomProbe` now prints the player's mark before and after each gesture.

- 09:52: the pinch gave `ZOOM mark pinch before=A after=A` and `scale=1`. The double tap gave `before=A after=A`, but its `scale` answer never reached Metro's log. That log's last line was written at 09:54. At 11:31 the app was still in Metro's `/json/list`, CDP closed with 1006 before reading anything, the "Open debugger to view warnings." banner was up, and the answer stood in the player's note: `PROBE zoom-doubletap-2457 scale=1 innerWidth=402 meta=width=device-width, initial-scale=1.0, maximum-scale=1.0, user-scalable=no`. So the page answered and its line never reached the log.
- 11:32, after a relaunch: pinch `before=A after=A`, `scale=1 innerWidth=402`; double tap `before=A after=A`, `scale=1 innerWidth=402`. Both landed on laid-out text, and Metro's log went on.

The vm test in `test/renderer/rules.test.ts` ("a pinch, run") runs `landed` and `dragged` against synthetic touches. With `if (pinched) return;` disabled, three of its five cases failed.

## Independent verification: a pinch during a Fish reading leaves the page following (2026-09-28 12:30)

An ios-tester run (glm-5.3-flash) on the same simulator and Metro, working tree `0.0.2-beta46`, with the report in `/tmp/rz-ios-tester-report.md`:

- **Gestures.** `zoom.sh` read, for both gestures, `scale=1 innerWidth=402` with the mark `before=A after=A`. The Safari control went from 1.00 to 2.68.
- **One finger still browses.** A one-finger drag (`FollowingProbe.testDragPageByParams`) still turned A into M. A tap on a sentence moved the reading from Utterance 2 to 7, highlighted it, and brought back A.
- **Long press still looks up.** `TranslationProbe.testLongPressDisabledThenEnabledDrawerAndCopy` passed, with the new passive `touchstart` beside lookup's own.
- **During a Fish reading** (voice Sarah, with Word Timings), a pinch left `scale=1` and the mark A, on the short fixture and on a real book (`Cultivation Online 2001-2044`).
- **Frame by frame across the pinch** (`line-follow.cjs arm`/`analyse`, 41 s): 20 line changes in section 2, each a 20 px glide of 233–261 ms (one of 653 ms) resting 0.2 px from the target, before and after the pinch at about 24.5 s. The recorder logged no page movement at the pinch. The one `other move`, at 27470 ms, was −722 px in steps `[-1401,503,176]`. It came as the views went 3 → 1 → 2 and ended with the Utterance's first line 0.2 px from the target: epub.js keeping the text still through a trim and a prepend, three seconds after the pinch.

Two `TranslationProbe.testSettingsDefaultsMenusAndPersistence` failures, at `:134` and `:153`, came from the tester's own setup. In the first, the long-press method had run first and left lookup on. In the second, a `settings` patch left in `harness.json` ran again on the probe's cold launch. Metro's log holds that `HX settings patched {"lookup":{"enabled":false,…}}`. With the file cleared, the method passed.
