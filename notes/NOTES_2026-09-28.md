The hold during playback is the scheduler's `allowed()`, which is false while the runtime's `playing` is set. With it bypassed by calling `playbackActive(false)` after Play, the download and the Reading ran together in the foreground at about the rate the download had alone. Locked, the same bypass kept the app running (the Reading played and CDP answered for the whole 120 s), but the chapter boundary stopped the download: its text preparation, done by the hidden `DownloadIndexer` rendering, did not complete in either locked run and hit the 60 s timeout in `indexer.tsx`, while in the foreground it completed within one sample. Nothing moved the `blocked` task on after the app came back.

The simulator's background time is not the phone's: here clips came at the usual rate for about 17 s after leaving the app and the task was marked `interrupted` at about 60 s. `lock-device.sh` takes about 15 s per call, so each `away (locked)` mark is a second or two after the lock itself.
